# Visitor record checks and their limits

Checked 20 October 2026. These checks identify record gaps. They do not certify legal compliance or decide who may enter a building.

## Collection notice: NOTICE

New Zealand collection rules require reasonable steps to inform people about collected information and its purpose. Indirect collection has separate requirements. [Office of the Privacy Commissioner guidance](https://www.privacy.org.nz/resources-and-learning/knowledge-base/view/312/).

The CLI records the notice version and evidence reference. The operator must provide the actual notice and decide how indirect collection is handled. The check flags missing or old versions. Updating a notice does not mean past collection was unlawful; it creates a review task.

## Retention: RETENTION

New Zealand principle 9 limits retention to the period needed for a lawful purpose. [Official principle 9](https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/9/).

Australian APP entities must take reasonable steps to destroy or de-identify unneeded information, subject to statutory and other stated exceptions. [OAIC APP 11 guidance](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/australian-privacy-principles/australian-privacy-principles-guidelines/chapter-11-app-11-security-of-personal-information).

Each site chooses a review period. The demo's 90 days is a business-policy example, not a period set by either law. The retention view flags due records. Redaction needs explicit confirmation and is blocked by a hold, current presence or an unexpired review period. It removes visitor name, email, company, purpose and evidence reference from that visit. Dates, site, host and action codes remain and can still support identification. This is field redaction, not guaranteed de-identification. Include exports, generated documents, drafts and backups in the approved disposal process.

## Site induction: INDUCTION

This is an operator policy, not a statutory rule. The CLI records the version and evidence reference. No check proves that the visitor understood an induction. Retain the actual evidence separately. Current local check-in requires both notice and induction evidence.

## Host: HOST

This is an operator policy. A current visit needs an available host at the same site. The check flags unavailable hosts. Approval actor labels are not authenticated identities; protected operator access is required. No identity, sanctions or legal screening is performed.

## Operational boundary

This software keeps reception records. It does not operate doors, issue legal signatures or deliver emergency alerts. The onsite list is a snapshot, not proof of physical presence. The site emergency plan and staff reconciliation remain authoritative. A shared deployment needs authenticated access, backups and controls on generated files.
